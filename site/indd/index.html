BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Built for access.
Honest about fidelity.

This independent patch backports the open-source INDD importer to DesignCraft 0.8.0. It converts the document in memory and opens it through DesignCraft’s IDML importer.

  • Fonts and images matter. Keep the original Links and Document Fonts folders beside your document when available. Embedded previews can help with missing images, but aren’t a replacement for print-quality originals.
  • Layouts can change. Some formatting, text flow and advanced InDesign features may not survive import exactly.
  • Import, not INDD export. Save to DesignCraft or IDML; this patch cannot write Adobe’s INDD format.
  • Independent test builds. Installers may be unsigned. Verify the publisher, release notes and SHA-256 checksums before installation. No security settings are changed by this installer.

A few useful answers.

Why doesn’t my current DesignCraft open INDD?

The published 0.8.0 build predates the direct INDD integration. This package backports that integration into a separately installed build.

Can I use this on my phone or tablet?

These installers are for desktop macOS, Windows and Linux. No iOS or Android installer is provided.

Why didn’t the download start automatically?

Your browser may require a click, the processor may need confirmation, or a matching installer may not yet be published. Choose the correct system and processor, then use the download button. We never redirect you to a build for a different platform.

Is this an official Adobe or ArtCraft release?

No. This is an independent distribution by irajeev.com. DesignCraft and indd-utils are open-source projects; their authors are credited in the repository.

How do I remove the patched edition?

On macOS, remove DesignCraft iRajeev from Applications. On Windows, use Installed apps → DesignCraft iRajeev → Uninstall. On Debian/Ubuntu, remove the irajeev-designcraft-indd package. Your original DesignCraft app is separate.
